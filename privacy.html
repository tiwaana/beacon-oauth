Beacon Privacy Policy

Beacon accesses only Google accounts whose holders authorize it. Data may include email, calendar, files and other Google services the holder authorizes. Data is stored on the owner's computer and used only to run the assistant. Parts are processed by or sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic), Notion (tasks and notes) and Telegram (alerts). Data is not sold or used for advertising. Holders can revoke access in their Google Account permissions at any time.
